Stock-out escalation SOP

The process behind the red cells, written so it runs the same way every week.

  1. Monday 9:00, refresh. Load board sell-through and inventory reports, co-packer shipment logs and open POs. Reconcile before anything is reported.
  2. Flag. Any SKU under 3 weeks of supply, or under 5 weeks with no on-order landing in time, goes on the action list.
  3. Check the pipeline. Confirm PO quantity and date with the co-packer in writing. Short-fill or slipped date: escalate the same day.
  4. Rebalance. If a board is overstocked on the same SKU, propose reallocating the next order before placing a new one.
  5. Close the loop. Commercial Coordinator and sales get the list by noon. Every item has an owner and a date, and stays on the list until it is resolved.

Definitions

How every number on this page is calculated.

Weeks of supply (WOS): on-hand units ÷ average weekly sell-through over the last 4 weeks.
Coverage: (on-hand + on-order) ÷ average weekly sell-through. Drives the overstock flag.
Stock-out risk: WOS under 3, and either nothing on order or the PO lands after projected stock-out.
Fill rate: units received ÷ units ordered on POs due that week.
On-time delivery: POs fully received on or before the confirmed date ÷ POs due.
Forecast: 4-week moving average with a trend adjustment; accuracy shown as MAPE against actuals.
